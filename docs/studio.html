The studio

A small studio with a big allergy to mediocre software.

shift-tab started as a lab. Years of building on monday.com kept surfacing the same thought: the tools we wished existed didn't. So we built them, published them, and the lab became the company.

Today that's two live products — Monday.com Inspector on the Chrome Web Store and MondayVirtual — with the next one already on the bench. Between releases, we take on a small number of client builds: AI pilots, custom products, monday.com platform work.

01

Products, not projects

We ship and maintain our own software. Client work is held to the same bar — published-product quality, not deliverable quality.

02

The last 2%

Empty states, error copy, the way a window settles. The difference between software people use and software people remember lives there.

03

Shortcuts respected

Tools for people who know their shortcuts: keyboard-first, fast by default, nothing between intent and action.
